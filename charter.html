Governing Document

The Founding Charter

Status: Private Academic & Technical Organization — pre-incorporation, minor-operated with guardian oversight. Version: Charter v1.0.

Article I

Name & Identity

The organization operates under the working name The Logan Group (also "the Organization"). The final legal name may be amended upon formal incorporation.

Article II

Purpose & Mission

The Logan Group exists to unify education, research, and engineering into a single, disciplined system that prioritizes learning, depth, and long-term impact over short-term profit.

  • EducateDesign and operate independent educational platforms.
  • ResearchConduct technical and interdisciplinary research.
  • BuildBuild high-quality software and systems.
  • DocumentRecord knowledge for long-term use.
  • PreparePosition for future academic, institutional, and societal impact.
Article III

Core Principles

  • 01 · Learning FirstKnowledge and understanding take priority over monetization.
  • 02 · Depth Over BreadthFewer projects done deeply are superior to many shallow efforts.
  • 03 · Documentation as ProofAll serious work must be documented clearly and truthfully.
  • 04 · Long-Term ThinkingDecisions are evaluated for multi-year or multi-decade impact.
  • 05 · IntegrityNo misrepresentation of work, credentials, or outcomes.
  • 06 · SustainabilityNo burnout, no reckless expansion, no unsound financial behavior.
Article IV

Organizational Structure

The Organization functions as an umbrella structure. The Parent Organization owns and governs all divisions, and sets standards, policies, and long-term direction. It does not sell directly — all work flows through a division.

Explore each division in depth on its own page: Jones Academy, Logan Labs, Logan Works, Logan Press, Logan Research, Logan Infrastructure, Logan Ventures, and The Upper Room Ministry.

Article V

Governance & Oversight

While the Founder is a minor: a parent or legal guardian provides oversight where required, and all financial and legal actions require approval. Strategic and academic decisions are made by the Founder; legal and financial decisions require guardian consent. There is no hidden financial activity, no undisclosed partnerships, and no misrepresentation to institutions.

Article VI

Financial Philosophy

Monetization is optional, not mandatory. Learning and credibility precede revenue. No leverage, debt, or speculative behavior. Any future revenue must support sustainability and quality.

Rule: the Organization must remain valuable even if it generates no profit.

Article VII

Academic & Ethical Standards

Original work only, with proper attribution when referencing others. Respect for academic honesty and ethical consideration in technology and education. Violations of these standards invalidate the Organization's mission.

Article VIII

Intellectual Property

All work is owned by the Organization unless otherwise specified. Licensing decisions are made intentionally, and open-source and closed-source models may coexist.

Rule: intellectual property exists to preserve integrity, not restrict learning.

Article IX

Future Incorporation

When legally eligible, the Organization may incorporate as an LLC, nonprofit, or hybrid, and existing divisions become formal entities or brands. This charter may be amended but not contradicted — every amendment must preserve the mission, the principles, and the long-term orientation.

Article X

Amendments

This charter may be amended when changes align with the core mission, when there is no dilution of academic or ethical standards, and with guardian approval where legally required.

Article XI

Adoption

This charter is adopted as the foundational governing document of The Logan Group.

Articles XII–XX define each division individually, and the ministry addendum (Article XXI) covers The Upper Room Ministry. Rather than repeat them here, each has its own page — linked from the divisions grid on the home page.
