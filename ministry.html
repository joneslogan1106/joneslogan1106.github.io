Addendum · Article XXI — The Logan Group Founding Charter
Spiritual & Devotional Arm

The Upper Room Ministry

The engine that gives the whole system its “why.”

The Upper Room Ministry provides daily scriptural teaching, theological reflection, and spiritual discipline grounded in a non-denominational Christian tradition — serving as both personal practice and public ministry. It is the ethical foundation the rest of The Logan Group is built on, kept clearly separate from academic study of religion.

Read the full Charter
Purpose

Why The Upper Room Ministry exists

  • 01Maintain a non-negotiable daily devotional practice
  • 02Publish sermons, reflections, and theological writing to a public audience
  • 03Provide the moral and ethical framework the other divisions operate within
  • 04Build community, starting from a local network and growing deliberately
Scope

Daily Rhythm

01

Morning Study

Scripture and commentary — roughly twenty minutes, before academic or work sessions begin.

02

Reflection

Personal application of that morning's study.

03

Recording / Writing

The sermon or devotional itself is produced.

04

Publishing

Shared to the ministry platform and, where relevant, social media.

Long-Term Vision

Where The Upper Room Ministry is going

  • Phase 1 — Now

    Personal practice and a simple digital presence, integrated into the daily system.

  • Phase 2 — College Years

    Leading a small group, formal teaching curriculum, higher-production content.

  • Phase 3 — Post-College

    Possible 501(c)(3) incorporation, publishing partnerships, and speaking.

  • Phase 4 — Long-Term

    A recognized spiritual pillar of The Logan Group, cross-pollinating with Academy religious studies and Press theological research.

Guardrails

Boundaries this ministry keeps

  • Rule 01Stay grounded in core, historic Christian conviction without tying it to any single denomination
  • Rule 02Keep devotional application distinct from academic study
  • Rule 03Avoid sectarian polemics that would narrow the ministry's reach
  • Rule 04Lead with humility — “here's what I'm learning,” not “here's absolute truth”
  • Rule 05Ministry funds are never drawn from investment layers, and never the reverse
