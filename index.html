Article II — Mission

Learning first. Everything else follows.

The Logan Group exists to design independent educational platforms, conduct interdisciplinary research, build high-quality software and systems, and document knowledge for long-term use — preparing for real academic and institutional impact rather than chasing short-term gain.

  • Learning firstKnowledge and understanding take priority over monetization.
  • Depth over breadthFewer projects done deeply beats many done shallowly.
  • Documentation as proofAll serious work is documented clearly and truthfully.
  • Long-term thinkingDecisions are weighed on multi-year, multi-decade impact.
  • SustainabilityNo burnout, no reckless expansion, no unsound finances.

Article V — Governance

Overseen, transparent, accountable

While the Founder is a minor, a parent or legal guardian provides oversight on every financial and legal decision. Strategic and academic direction stays with the Founder. There is no hidden financial activity, no undisclosed partnerships, and no misrepresentation to institutions — by rule, not by convenience.

Read the full governance charter →
  • DecisionsAcademic strategy: Founder. Legal & financial: guardian consent required.
  • MonetizationOptional, never mandatory. Learning and credibility precede revenue.
  • VenturesCommercialization stays dormant until legal eligibility and Charter review.
