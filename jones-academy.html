Article XII — The Logan Group Founding Charter
Flagship Educational Institution

Jones Academy

Free education is a right. Advanced mastery is a premium.

Jones Academy provides universal, high-quality education at no cost, organized into four schools modeled on a modern research university. Long-term operation is sustained only through optional, advanced tools that enhance — but never gate — access to knowledge.

Read the full Charter
Purpose

Why Jones Academy exists

  • 01Design curriculum with the rigor of a research university, not a course platform
  • 02Keep every core subject free and permanently accessible
  • 03Fund continued operation through optional Mastery Tools, never through paywalls on fundamentals
Scope

Four Schools

01

School of Mathematics & Logic

Algebra through analysis, discrete mathematics, statistics, logic, and mathematical physics.

02

School of Applied Science & Engineering

Physics, chemistry, biology, and computer science taught through computational, systems-based methods.

03

School of Humanities & Social Sciences

History, philosophy, psychology, economics, political science, and religious & ethical studies.

04

School of Languages, Arts & Skillcraft

World languages, fine arts, and practical, technical craft.

Long-Term Vision

Where Jones Academy is going

  • Now

    Private, self-directed institute — depth and documentation over scale.

  • Near-term

    A public library of free coursework across all four schools.

  • Long-term

    A globally accessible digital university and permanent knowledge institution.
