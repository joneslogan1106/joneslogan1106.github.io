# The Power of God Through Your Weaknesses
---
## INFO
**Passage of the Day**: Job 25
**Verses**: 2 Corinthians 12:9, Isaiah 40:29-31, Philippians 4:13, Ephesians 3:20

---
## Morning Greeting
Good morning, I hope we are currently having a blessed day as we all start to wake up. As you start today it is important you put God at the forefront. In order to do that today, we'll dive into his word. As a quick overview of what we're going to go over today, we are going to look at the power of **you** *and* **God**. Many people have heard about His power and His saving us from sin; but do you know where you stand in relation to His power? Today we'll explore all of this, by first going into prayer and silencing our minds.
### Prayer
Dear Heavenly Father,
I come before you today to ask for silence of my mind O God. I ask that you allow me to see your power today O God, and I ask that you allow me to understand your text O God. Thank you for giving me the gift of allowing me to study your word, and allow me to understand the depth of your power today.
In Jesus' mighty name I pray Amen.

---
## Movement to Scripture
Today's text calls us to reflect on the Lord's power. In Job 25 it's a short speech from one of Job's friends known as Bildad who visited during his suffering. It's a powerful speech, which tells us the extent of God's power. He is the holder of **divine authority**, and that authority supersedes all authority.

We as humans as humans can never compare to his power, we were created with weaknesses. The Lord knows this about us and He calls us every day to be a part of His power, but it's up to **us** to accept his calling. For those who belong to Christ, we possess the Holy Spirit within us, but it surfaces in different ways for each person in ways known as "gifts."

These gifts allow us to use the Lord's power, and spread his word in a variety of ways. We all have a part in his will, it will just take time to find your part. The Lord will reveal your gift in His timing, you just need to be aware. You always need to listen and look, because your calling can be right in front of you it's only a matter of whether your eyes are open.

In this passage we are also told about God's dominion. His dominion encompasses all of us, all life, and everything that exists. His order creates, sustains, unites, and ultimately ends all life. We are protected by his dominion, because as humans, we ***are*** impure. You cannot come from man, and be perfectly pure. Yes, you can become pure but you can only become pure from God's power.

---
## Closing
His dominion and his power supply us with everything. We are inside of his power, and we serve him in many different ways but he uses us in order to complete his will. His will will carry us through any circumstances, and through him we can do anything.
