Article XV — The Logan Group Founding Charter
Publishing & Documentation Arm

Logan Press

The permanent, citable record of everything The Logan Group produces.

Logan Press formally publishes, preserves, and distributes the intellectual work produced across every division — white papers, research notes, textbooks, and engineering post-mortems — to a standard of long-term archival quality.

Read the full Charter
Purpose

Why Logan Press exists

  • 01Hold every division to the same standard of sourcing and structure
  • 02Preserve work against loss, in formats built to outlast their format (LaTeX, plain text, open archives)
  • 03Give the whole organization one publishing arm, not seven inconsistent ones
Scope

What Logan Press Publishes

01

Academic White Papers

History, science, geography, and economics papers built on the standard LaTeX templates.

02

Research Notes & Technical Reports

Findings from Logan Research fellowships and Logan Labs projects.

03

Textbooks & Learning Materials

Longer-form instructional works originating in Jones Academy.

04

Engineering Documentation

Post-mortems and technical write-ups from Logan Works and Logan Labs.
