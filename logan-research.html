Article XVI — The Logan Group Founding Charter
Research Programs & Fellowships

Logan Research

Structured inquiry, not scattered curiosity.

Logan Research cultivates deep, structured inquiry across mathematics, science, engineering, and history. Every project runs as a formal proposal with milestones, a conclusion, and an honest account of its limitations.

Read the full Charter
Purpose

Why Logan Research exists

  • 01Prepare participants for university-level and doctoral research
  • 02Require reflection and stated limitations as a mandatory part of every project — not just results
  • 03Route every finished finding through Logan Press for formal publication
Scope

How Research Runs

01

Tracks by Discipline

Independent research tracks opened per subject area as questions warrant.

02

The Fellowship Model

Participants operate as Research Fellows, with the same expectations as any working researcher.

03

Formal Proposals

Every project states its question, method, and milestones before work begins.

04

Peer Review

Internal review always; external review where feasible.
