Article XIV — The Logan Group Founding Charter
Application & Game Studio

Logan Works

Research → Education → Product → Feedback.

Logan Works builds and ships applications and games that put technical knowledge and systems thinking in front of real users. Nothing ships without documentation, testing, and reflection.

Read the full Charter
Purpose

Why Logan Works exists

  • 01Turn research and learning into software people actually use
  • 02Treat user experience as a design constraint, not an afterthought
  • 03Feed real-world usage data back into research and education
Scope

Product Lines

01

Applications

Educational apps, productivity tools, data-visualization tools, and utilities.

02

Games

Educational, simulation-based, and systems-driven games and experimental interactive media.

03

Platforms

Web, desktop, mobile, and cross-platform builds — the stack follows the product, not the trend.

Long-Term Vision

Where Logan Works is going

  • Now

    Private or educational builds — no aggressive marketing.

  • Near-term

    A small catalog of tested, documented applications and games.

  • Long-term

    A professional studio bridging research and real-world impact.
